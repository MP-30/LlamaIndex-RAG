Acme Corp — Employee Handbook Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-014 | Effective Date: 1 April 2026 | Version 2.1
1. Leave Policy
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited on 1 January.
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance and require
manager approval. Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the next
calendar year; any balance beyond this is forfeited. Employees are additionally entitled to 10 days of paid sick
leave per year, and a medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
2. Work Hours and Remote Work
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break. Employees
may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval, and must be reachable on company
communication tools during core hours (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM).
3. Notice Period and Resignation
Employees who wish to resign must submit a written notice of resignation with a minimum notice period of 60
days for confirmed employees and 15 days for employees still in their probation period. The notice period may
be waived or reduced at the sole discretion of management, subject to a valid business justification.
4. Reimbursement Policy
Employees can claim reimbursement for approved business expenses (travel, client meals, internet
allowance) by submitting receipts through the Finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Claims submitted after 30 days will not be processed. The monthly internet allowance for remote work is
capped at INR 1,500.
5. Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct, confidentiality of company and client data, and
a harassment-free workplace. Violations should be reported to HR via the confidential grievance email at
hr-grievance@acmecorp.example, and will be investigated within 10 working days of the report being filed.
